How it plays

Every room is a machine you finish

Each room is a cross-section of a clockwork cabinet: floors, gaps, lifts, presses and gear trains. The furnace is somewhere you can see. Getting there is the puzzle.

STEP 01 // TINKER

Plan with time frozen

Drag brass Job Cards into the room, even into mid-air, and hang gears, belts and steam pipes on the back wall. Nothing moves until you say so, and you can rearrange as often as you like.

STEP 02 // RUN

Pull the lever

The machinery spins up and the Courier sets off. From here you only watch, or tap to speed things up. All the skill is in the plan.

PASSED
STEP 03 // DELIVER

Stamp the docket

Get the Sun Spark home and a delivery docket feeds out, stamped PASSED. Solve it with the fewest pieces possible for all three brass cogs.

Why it sticks

A puzzle you can watch yourself solve

No reflexes, no timers, no guessing what went wrong. Just a room, a few pieces, and one satisfying run when everything lines up.

Every room has a best answer

Each of the 400 rooms was solved exhaustively by computer to find the true fewest pieces. Match it for three cogs. One over earns two. Any delivery earns one.

Failure shows you why

When a plan goes wrong you see exactly where: a fall, a blade, a press. The Courier rattles apart, your pieces stay where you left them, and a faint trail shows the path it took.

Tap anything to learn it

Unsure what a winch, a vent or a crumbling floor does? Tap it. A card opens with a little looping demo, run by the same rules as the room itself.

Machinery that really runs

Gears reverse at every mesh, ratio gears double the speed, crossed belts flip direction and two drives forced to fight jam the whole train. The rules are simple; the rooms are not.

A music box for every wing

A wind-up waltz plays in time with the Courier's steps, in a different key and colour for each wing. Every sound in the game is built from scratch.

Earn your finish

Cogs unlock new gear finishes and paint for the Courier, from Nickel and Rose Gold to Mother of Pearl. Earned by playing, never sold.

The toolbox

Small set of pieces. Endless ways to combine them.

Each card works once, wherever the Courier first meets it. Each part goes on the room's back wall. Every one is taught by a room of its own before it's mixed with the rest.
