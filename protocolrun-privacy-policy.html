Privacy Policy

Effective 21 August 2026  ·  Last updated 21 August 2026
Applies to the Protocol:RUN Android application (com.protocolrun.game)

The short version

Protocol:RUN has no accounts, no logins and no servers of its own. Your progress is saved only on your device and is never uploaded. I run no analytics and I collect nothing about you.

The one exception is advertising. The game shows ads through Google AdMob, and AdMob collects device and advertising identifiers to serve and measure them. If you buy the Supporter unlock, Google Play handles the payment and I never see your payment details.

Everything below is the long version of those three paragraphs.

01Who this is

Protocol:RUN is developed and published by Andy Marcus ("I", "me", "the developer"), an independent developer. For the purposes of the UK GDPR and EU GDPR, I am the data controller for the limited processing described in this policy.

Contact: protocolrun@andymarcus.com

02What I collect myself

Nothing. That is not a simplification. Protocol:RUN has no user accounts, no sign-in, no leaderboard server, no crash reporting and no analytics of any kind. There is no back end for the game to send anything to.

Your saved game — best scores, distances, credits, upgrades, cosmetics, contract progress, daily streaks, settings and ghost replays — is written to a single file in the app's private storage on your device (protocol_run.save). It never leaves the device, it is not backed up to me, and I have no way to read it. Uninstalling the game deletes it.

03What third parties collect

The game includes Google's advertising and billing components. These are operated by Google, not by me, and they process data under Google's own policies.

Google AdMob — advertising

Protocol:RUN shows two kinds of ad: a rewarded ad you may choose to watch in exchange for continuing a run, and an occasional full-screen ad between runs. To serve and measure these, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process:

  • your device's advertising identifier (Android Advertising ID);
  • device and app information — model, operating system version, app version, language, network type;
  • approximate location inferred from your IP address (country/region level; the game requests no GPS or location permission);
  • interactions with ads — whether an ad was shown, viewed, completed or clicked.

This is used to select ads, limit how often you see the same one, measure performance, and detect invalid or fraudulent traffic. Read Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses data from apps that use its services.

Google Play Billing — purchases

The game offers one optional purchase, the Supporter unlock, which removes the between-runs ads. Payment is handled entirely by Google Play. I receive only confirmation that a purchase for my product identifier succeeded. I never receive or store your name, card number, billing address or any other payment detail.

Google Play Services

The game is distributed through Google Play and relies on Google Play Services on your device. Google may process data in connection with app distribution, updates and integrity checks independently of this policy.

06Your choices

Consent, in the EEA, UK and Switzerland

Before any personalised ad is requested, the game shows a Google-certified consent message using Google's User Messaging Platform. You may refuse personalised ads; the game remains fully playable, and you will see non-personalised ads instead.

On your device, anywhere

  • Opt out of ad personalisation: Android Settings → Google → All services → Ads, then "Delete advertising ID" or "Opt out of Ads Personalisation".
  • Reset your advertising ID in the same place, which disconnects future ad data from past ad data.
  • Remove the between-runs ads by buying the Supporter unlock. Rewarded ads remain available because they are always your choice to start.
  • Delete everything by uninstalling the game, which removes the local save file.

07Legal bases for processing

Where the UK or EU GDPR applies:

  • Consent (Art. 6(1)(a)) — personalised advertising, and any use of the advertising identifier for it.
  • Contract (Art. 6(1)(b)) — delivering a purchase you have made.
  • Legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f)) — serving non-personalised ads, capping how often ads appear, and preventing ad fraud.

08Retention and international transfers

I hold no personal data, so I have nothing to retain or delete on request. Data processed by Google is retained under Google's own schedules, described in its privacy policy.

Google operates globally and may process data outside your country, including in the United States, relying on transfer mechanisms such as the EU Standard Contractual Clauses.

09Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or port your personal data, to object to or restrict processing, and to withdraw consent. Because the data described here is held by Google rather than by me, requests are usually best made to Google directly — but write to me and I will help you route them.

If you are in the EEA or UK you may also complain to your national data protection authority. In the UK that is the Information Commissioner's Office.

If you are a California resident: I do not sell or share personal information as those terms are defined by the CCPA/CPRA. Ad personalisation may qualify as "sharing" for cross-context behavioural advertising, which you can turn off using the device controls in section 06.

10Children

Protocol:RUN is rated for players aged 13 and over and is not directed to children. It is not enrolled in Google Play's Designed for Families programme. I do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information through this app, contact me and I will act on it.
