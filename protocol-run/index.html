Take your run off the road

Five worlds to earn

Start on the Neon Grid and unlock The Stack, Tideworks, The Crucible and The Null. Each world has its own routes, atmosphere and signature hazards. Earned with stars, never bought.

03

180 contracts, three stars each

Finite runs on fixed seeds that you can learn and master. Finish the route, beat its score target, chase a clean clear. Nine hundred metres of subway and nine hundred of rooftop are two different levels.

04

Your toughest rival is you

Replay Daily runs and Contracts against a ghost of your own best line. See where you gained ground, where you lost it, and where your next record is coming from.

05

A fresh reason to run

A new Daily seed in every world, the same for everyone until UTC midnight. Build a streak. Take on the five-challenge Weekly Circuit and stamp your way to the Relay colourway, finish and trail.

06

Build your style

Collect credits, upgrade power-up duration and payouts, and customise your runner with colourways, suit finishes and trails. Shield up, pull in credits with the Data Magnet, or smash through with Overclock.
